Page-by-page text summary

SCOPE AND READING NOTES

This document covers 26 accessible pages: the supplied landing page and 25
pages discovered beneath https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/.
Discovery followed the section's landing pages, supplier and expert profiles,
related-page links, and supplementary web searches. URLs are deduplicated;
query-string variants are not counted separately.

Each entry contains the page's visible main title, source URL, a heading
outline, and an original summary of its substantive readable content.
Heading outlines are paraphrased rather than a verbatim heading inventory.
Summaries describe McDonald's statements; they are not independent verification
of the company's sourcing, nutrition, sustainability, or historical claims.
Historical dates remain attached to the claims that use them.

Coverage is complete for the accessible pages discovered in this review, but
the site's complete URL inventory could not be verified: its sitemap was not
retrievable. Unlinked or unindexed pages may exist. The nutrition calculator
and questions interface did not expose their dynamically loaded results in
the retrieved text. Videos, image-only text, and downloadable files were not
transcribed. These limits are documented rather than filled with assumptions.

The boundary excludes /product/, /full-menu/, /faq/, corporate.mcdonalds.com,
other countries and languages, external sites, and linked PDFs outside the
specified path. Related destinations are identified where relevant.

Shared site navigation and notices are summarized once at the end.

01. About Our Food
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food.html

Heading outline: Food overview; quality commitments; ingredients; food
principles; nutrition tool; food specialists; fresh beef; children's meals;
range of options.

Summary:
The landing page introduces ingredient, dietary, calorie, and nutrition
information. It presents a more balanced Happy Meal and cooked-to-order
Quarter Pounder beef as examples of menu development. Eight cards lead to
quality and sourcing policies, ingredient information, food philosophy, the
nutrition calculator, food experts, the fresh-beef lineup, children's
nutrition, and menu variety. Experts include chefs, dietitians, and suppliers.
The quality card emphasizes changes to preparation and ingredients; the
ingredients card invites closer examination of food components.

Fresh-beef claims concern Quarter Pounder patties, not every burger. The
quarter-pound weight is four ounces before cooking. Availability is at
participating restaurants, mainly in the contiguous United States, and
excludes Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories.

02. Our Food Philosophy
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/our-food-philosophy.html

Heading outline: Chicken nuggets; quality improvements; nutrition tool;
ingredient information; supplier profiles; customer food questions.

Summary:
McDonald's frames its food principles around safety, quality, choice, and
responsible production. Examples include pure beef patties seasoned with salt
and pepper, freshly cracked eggs in Egg McMuffins, U.S. Filet-O-Fish whitefish
from managed wild fisheries, and white-meat chicken nuggets. It describes
continued menu improvement as a response to customer priorities.

The nugget card states that Chicken McNuggets contain no artificial colors,
preservatives, or flavors. Other cards direct readers to quality commitments,
the nutrition calculator, ingredient information, supplier stories, and food
questions. The nugget link opens a product page outside this document's path
boundary. The customer-question link opens the general FAQ section, also
outside the boundary. This page is primarily an overview and navigation hub,
not a complete ingredient list for every menu item.

05. Nutrition Calculator
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/nutrition-calculator.html

Heading outline: Nutrition calculator; daily-value footnotes; ingredient,
allergy, and nutrition qualifications.

Summary:
The tool is intended to show calories, carbohydrates, protein, and other
product nutrition data. Its interactive product results were not present
in the retrieved text.

The accompanying note identifies nine allergens: eggs, dairy, wheat, soy,
peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish, and sesame. Shared preparation spaces,
equipment, and utensils can allow allergen contact despite precautions.
Customers are directed to ingredient information, medical advice for dietary
questions, and contact forms.

Daily values assume a 2,000-calorie diet, with individual needs varying;
percentages and reference intakes use unrounded values. Nutrition data come
from accredited laboratory tests, published resources, and suppliers, using
standard formulations and serving sizes. Reported values are averages rounded
under FDA rules. Preparation, portion sizes, suppliers, location, season,
testing, and recipe changes can alter actual values. Fountain-drink figures
assume standard fill levels with ice; self-service fountain signs provide
figures without ice. Beverage sizes can vary by market.

The page makes no U.S. Halal, Kosher, other religious-compliance, vegetarian,
vegan, or gluten-free claims. Its general information note is dated January
2022 unless otherwise indicated.

06. Try Our Fresh Beef* Lineup
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/fresh-beef.html

Heading outline: Fresh-beef positioning; bacon version; deluxe version;
standard cheese version; double version.

Summary:
The page describes Quarter Pounder patties as entirely fresh beef, cooked
when ordered on a flat grill and seasoned with salt and pepper.

The standard cheese burger includes two cheese slices, slivered onions,
pickles, ketchup, and mustard on a toasted sesame bun. The bacon version adds
thick-cut Applewood-smoked bacon to beef, two American-cheese slices, onions,
and pickles on a sesame bun. The deluxe version adds shredded lettuce, three
Roma-tomato slices, and mayonnaise, alongside cheese, onions, and pickles.
The double version uses two fresh patties, with two cheese slices and the
standard onion, pickle, ketchup, and mustard toppings.

The stated four-ounce weight is before cooking, and applies to each patty
in the double burger. Fresh beef is available at most contiguous-U.S.
restaurants, excluding Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories. Product links
lead outside the path boundary. Ordering guidance points to restaurant
search, app pickup, and delivery. Delivery is available at participating
restaurants, may cost more, and may involve additional fees.

07. Kids’ Nutrition
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/whats-in-your-food/kids-nutrition.html

Heading outline: Children's meals introduction; balanced meal development;
impact of nutrition initiatives.

Summary:
McDonald's describes adapting Happy Meals to different families and making
balanced choices more accessible. U.S. changes began in 2004 with apple
dippers, milk, and apple juice in selected markets; apple slices became
automatic inclusions in 2012. It also describes removing artificial
preservatives from nuggets and hamburgers, with a pickle exception.

Reduced-sugar low-fat chocolate milk arrived in early 2020. The page claims
25% less sugar than leading quick-service competitors: 14 grams versus 23.

Work with the Alliance for a Healthier Generation began in 2013. Removing
soda from U.S. Happy Meal menu boards in 2014 is associated on the page with
a 15% increase in orders with milk, water, or juice drinks from 2013 to 2018.
Global goals were announced in 2018. The page reports over 2.5 billion
qualifying items sold since 2018 across 20 markets: Argentina, Australia,
Austria, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Italy, Japan,
Netherlands, Poland, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan, U.K., and U.S.
Qualifying categories include produce, low-fat dairy, water, lean protein,
and whole grains. The reporting endpoint is unspecified.

12. Chef Chad Schafer
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/meet-our-food-experts/our-chefs/chef-chad-schafer.html

Heading outline: Career start; training and experience; food principles;
motivation and alternate career; daily work; menu priorities; customer
questions; team surprises; long-term aims; favorite item; home cooking.

Summary:
Schafer's first paid restaurant position was grilling at Applebee's. He
graduated at the top of his Culinary Institute of America class. His account
describes ten years in independent restaurants, followed by ten years as
a corporate chef at Schreiber, Givaudan Flavors and Fragrances, and Cargill.

His approach favors simplicity, enjoyment in cooking, and respect for
ingredients and the work behind them. Coworkers are a major motivation;
he views McDonald's scale as an opportunity to influence perceptions of
food and the people making it. Without cooking, he imagines still seeking
his purpose.

Daily work resembles other restaurant culinary work, but decisions affect
more people. He is excited by quality improvements and attention to
customer preferences. Customers often ask about discontinued favorites.
He thinks the existence and diversity of the culinary team surprise people.
His long-term aim is continued improvement through menu evolution.
His favorite menu item is whatever the team is developing. At home,
he enjoys making food that visibly pleases friends and family.

13. Chef Mike Lingo
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/meet-our-food-experts/our-chefs/chef-mike-lingo.html

Heading outline: Career start; education and development work; food
principles; motivation and alternate career; daily tasks; menu direction;
customer questions; culinary team; favorite item; home cooking.

Summary:
Lingo began at age 14 in his father's grocery-store deli. After high school
he studied culinary arts in Cedar Rapids and worked in an Italian
restaurant. Encouragement from its chef led him to the Culinary Institute
of America in New York, where he also worked at Terrapin in Rhinebeck.
He later entered research and development in Iowa and enrolled in food
science at Iowa State University.

He values enjoyable food, shared meals, creativity, and experimentation.
At McDonald's, menu rollout requires coordinating many restaurants and
suppliers, maintaining consistent quality, and ensuring sufficient
ingredient supplies. Successful launches are therefore rewarding.
An alternative career would involve design, architecture, landscaping,
or interiors.

Daily work can involve creating sandwiches, developing supplier ingredients,
or presenting concepts to leadership. He welcomes a return to the
company's roots. People often propose menu additions; he thinks the number
of trained chefs would surprise them. His favorite product is the Big Mac.
For family and friends, he enjoys holiday roasts, braises, stews, and other
comfort foods.

16. Keystone Foods
======================================================================
URL: https://www.mcdonalds.com/us/en-us/about-our-food/meet-our-suppliers/keystone-foods.html

Heading outline: Company history; 100 Circle Farms; Dietrich orchard;
Longaker fish supply; food questions; balanced choices; supplier directory.

Summary:
The story traces Keystone to a small Philadelphia family beef business.
During the 1960s, Herb Lotman developed an individually quick-frozen patty
process to preserve taste and texture, then introduced it to McDonald's.
The resulting contract supported Keystone's growth. The page describes
operations supplying food to 13 countries across multiple regions and
continued worldwide use of the freezing method.

In the early 1970s, Keystone and McDonald's developed consolidated
distribution so restaurants could receive operating supplies in a single
customized delivery. They later collaborated on Chicken McNuggets,
creating substantial demand for boneless chicken.

The profile reports annual U.S. supply of more than 150 million pounds
of beef, 300 million pounds of chicken, and 15 million pounds of fish.
It says Keystone meets McDonald's welfare, safety, and quality standards
beyond USDA requirements. These figures have no explicit current
reporting year in the readable story.

Related cards lead to potato, apple, and fish profiles, food questions,
balanced choices, and the supplier directory.

Heading outline: Fishing and sustainability story.

Longaker departs Dutch Harbor aboard the Defender to catch wild Alaska
pollock in the Bering Sea for Filet-O-Fish. The page describes pollock as

long-term availability.

His account says catches are filleted and frozen approximately 24 to 72
hours after capture to meet the company's quality expectations.
McDonald's associates the fish with sustainable sourcing through the
Alaska Seafood Marketing Institute and Marine Stewardship Council
certification. An external link directs readers to MSC information.

The main text focuses on the fishing process, prompt handling, and
sustainability standards. A return link leads to the supplier directory;
the product and certification links are outside this document's boundary.
